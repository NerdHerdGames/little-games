# Astronaut Academy

Four short stations teach preparation, teamwork, life in orbit, and observation through twelve picture choices. There is no timer or penalty. Hints and retries are unlimited, and each successful choice explains the lesson before the player advances. Completion shows a pretend training certificate; replay starts a fresh session. Training progress is not persisted.

Presentation uses project-owned inline SVG and a local SVG library thumbnail. Native buttons feed the shared `ActionMenu` and `ActionState`; the scene owns no device listeners. Saved mute and reduced-motion preferences apply to speech, feedback tones, and the gentle astronaut animation. Speech depends on an installed local browser voice.

Educational sources checked October 15, 2026:

- [NASA: What Is a Spacesuit?](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/what-is-a-spacesuit/)
- [NASA: Astronaut Exercise](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/iss-research/astronaut-exercise/)
- [NASA JPL: Imagine You're an Astronaut](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/edu/resources/project/imagine-youre-an-astronaut/)
- [NASA: Living and Working in Space](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/167746main_fs_livingandworkinginspace508c.pdf)

These are development references. Gameplay does not fetch them. This is simplified educational play, not an astronaut qualification or a real flight checklist.

Manual review: complete all stations with touch; check a wrong answer and hint; toggle sound and motion; replay; return to Games and re-enter; test narrow portrait and landscape tablet layouts. On Android, also verify installed/offline play and locally available speech.
