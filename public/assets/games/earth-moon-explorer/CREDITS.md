# Earth and Moon texture credits

Both textures are bundled locally; gameplay makes no requests to NASA or another external service. Downloaded October 14, 2026. These are NASA scientific visualizations used for an educational prototype, under [NASA's media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). No NASA insignia or endorsement is used.

## Earth — `earth-color.jpg`

- Credit: **NASA Earth Observatory**, Blue Marble: Next Generation, by Reto Stöckli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.
- Dataset: July 2004, cloud-free global base map.
- [Project and credits](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/)
- [Download collection](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/)
- [Source JPEG](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/july/world.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg)
- Adaptation: downsampled from 5400 × 2700 to 2048 × 1024 and JPEG encoded for tablet delivery. Geographic content is unchanged.

## Moon — `moon-color.jpg`

- Credit: **NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio**; visualization by Ernie Wright (USRA). LRO/LROC color mosaic data from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter camera team, Arizona State University; polar data from LRO/LOLA.
- Dataset: CGI Moon Kit, 2025 color map, 2048 × 1024 sRGB JPEG. This map is optimized for visual appearance, not scientific measurement.
- [Project, credits, and texture explanation](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/)
- [Source JPEG](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg)
- Adaptation: original 2K JPEG retained unchanged.

## Educational descriptions

Short descriptions in the viewer are paraphrased from [NASA's Earth facts](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/facts/) and [Moon facts](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/). Orbital distances, speeds, and lighting are simplified for a readable demonstration, not a scientific simulation.
